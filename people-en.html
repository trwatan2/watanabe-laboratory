01 / PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

PROFESSOR

Ryo Watanabe 渡部 綾

Professor, Chemical and Biological Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Shizuoka University

Research areas: catalysis, reaction engineering, catalytic processes and resource conversion. Current interests include structured catalysts, CO₂ conversion, electrified reactions and redox catalysis using lattice sulfur.

Alumnus of Yasushi Sekine’s laboratory at Waseda University. He studied applied chemistry from undergraduate through doctoral level. In the Sekine laboratory, he investigated redox catalysis involving interactions between lattice oxygen and steam. This experience led to his current work on lattice sulfur, CO₂ conversion and electrified catalytic processes.
