01 / VISION

Shizuoka University

Turning reactions into technologies for real-world use.

We connect catalysis, reaction engineering and electrified processes to develop new ways of converting CO₂, methane, hydrogen, carbon resources and sulfur compounds.

02 / Research

Research

Five research directions connect materials, reactor structures, energy supply, and carbon circulation.

01ELECTRIFIED
CATALYSISJOULE HEATING · e-DRM
01

ELECTRIFIED CATALYSIS

Electrified Catalysis

Direct Joule heating of structured catalysts to control where and how reaction heat is supplied, including electrified dry reforming and methanation.

Research details (Japanese) ↗
02CARBON
CIRCULATIONCO₂ → SYNGAS · SOLID CARBON
02

CARBON CIRCULATION

CO₂ Conversion & Solid Carbon

Reaction systems that convert CO₂ into syngas and solid carbon while coupling equilibrium control, carbon formation, separation and recovery.

Research details (Japanese) ↗
03STRUCTURED
CATALYSTSSPIRAL · POROUS METAL · FLOW
03

STRUCTURED CATALYSTS

Structured & Spiral Catalysts

Spiral structures, porous metals and washcoats are used to engineer flow, heat transfer and mass transfer together with catalytic activity.

Research details (Japanese) ↗
04e-REACTION
PROCESSELECTRICITY → CHEMICAL TRANSFORMATION
04

e-REACTION PROCESS

e-Reaction Processes

Electrified chemical processes that introduce electrical energy directly into catalysts and reactors for rapid response, local heating and compact operation.

Research details (Japanese) ↗
05LATTICE SULFUR
CATALYSISLATTICE S ⇄ VACANCY ⇄ REGENERATION
05

LATTICE SULFUR CATALYSIS

Lattice Sulfur Catalysis

Sulfide lattice sulfur is treated as a reactive species. Sulfur release, vacancy formation and H₂S re-sulfidation create redox catalytic cycles.
