REACTION ENGINEERING FOR A CARBON-CIRCULATING SOCIETY

Turning reactions into
technologies for real-world use.

We connect catalysis, reaction engineering and electrified reactions to develop new processes for CO₂, methane, hydrogen, carbon resources and sulfur compounds.

ELECTRIFIED CATALYSIS

Driving catalytic reactions with electricity.

We combine structured catalysts with electrical heating to control the reaction environment efficiently.

CARBON CIRCULATION

Turning CO₂ into resources.

03

STRUCTURED CATALYSTS

Making catalyst geometry work.

We use spiral structures, porous metals and washcoats to build catalytic reactors that account for heat and mass transfer.

04

e-REACTION PROCESS

Changing chemical processes through heat delivery.

We introduce electrical energy directly into reactors and catalysts, exploiting rapid response, local heating and compact equipment.

05LATTICE SULFUR
CATALYSISLATTICE S ⇄ VACANCY ⇄ REGENERATION
05

LATTICE SULFUR CATALYSIS

Using lattice sulfur
as a reactive species.

We study redox catalytic processes in which sulfur is released from sulfide lattices and reinserted from H₂S, repeatedly using lattice sulfur through sulfur vacancies.
